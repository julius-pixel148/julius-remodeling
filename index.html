FAQ

Questions.

Anything else, ask us at your consultation.

01Do you remodel single-family homes?

Yes. Most of our work is in houses across Seattle and the Eastside: kitchens, bathrooms, laundry rooms, flooring, and whole-home remodels. We handle demolition, plumbing, electrical, and mechanical work, and coordinate permits when required.

02What does "one team, one license, one budget" mean?

Your project runs through one coordinated team under one license, against one budget agreed before demolition. You don't manage separate trades or reconcile separate invoices, and change orders are documented and priced in writing.

03Can you handle a single room, or only full renovations?

Both. Whether you're renovating a single bathroom or reimagining an entire home, the process is the same: organized, transparent, and carefully executed.

04What happens at the free consultation?

We meet and discuss your ideas, needs, priorities, and budget. From there we develop the scope, design, material selections, and project plan, and you receive a clear proposal.

05Do you remodel condos and apartments too?

Yes. We handle the HOA approvals, certificates of insurance, elevator reservations, and work-hour rules that buildings require, before any work starts. See how condo and apartment remodeling works.

06Are you licensed, insured, and bonded?

Yes. Julius Remodeling Group is a licensed, insured, and bonded Washington general contractor (License JULIURG780NJ), and you can verify it with Washington L&I. Our subcontractors are licensed too and held to the same standard of accountability.

07Do you handle plumbing, electrical, and permits?

Yes. We manage demolition, plumbing, electrical, and mechanical work, and we coordinate permits whenever a project requires them. We also specialize in waterproofing, custom tile, detailed finishes, and fine trim and paint.

FAQ

Flooring questions.

Anything else, ask us at your consultation.

01Which flooring is best for kitchens and bathrooms?

Tile and luxury vinyl plank handle water best. Hardwood works beautifully in living spaces and bedrooms. We'll recommend options room by room.

02Do you remove the old flooring?

Yes. Removal, haul-away, and subfloor prep are part of the job, so the new floor goes down flat and solid.

03Can the same floor run through several rooms?

Yes. Running one floor through connected rooms makes a home feel larger and more cohesive, and we plan the layout so it flows.

04Do you do flooring as a standalone project?

Yes. Flooring can be its own project, or part of a kitchen, bathroom, basement, or whole-home remodel.

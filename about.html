About us

Family owned. Built on experience.

Julius Remodeling & Services is a family-owned company with over 15 years of experience in residential construction, remodeling, and home services.

10+Years in Greater Seattle

Our story

From Los Angeles to Seattle.

Our journey began in Los Angeles, where we worked with one of the area's largest construction companies.

There we gained hands-on experience in everything from basic home improvements to high-end custom renovations, ADU/DADU construction, garage conversions, additions, and complete home remodels.

Over a decade ago, we brought that experience to the Greater Seattle area. Today, with more than 1,500 completed projects, we proudly serve homeowners throughout Seattle, Bellevue, the Eastside, and surrounding communities.

How we work

Small on purpose. Personal by design.

We believe every successful project starts with understanding our customers' vision, needs, budget, and schedule. Our role is to help homeowners explore their options, find practical solutions, and turn their ideas into reality while making the process as straightforward and efficient as possible.

As a small, family-owned business, we maintain low overhead and an organized, efficient operation. We don't take on more projects than we can properly manage, so we stay personally involved and give each customer the time and attention they deserve.

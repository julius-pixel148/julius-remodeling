FAQ

Insulation questions.

Anything else, ask us at your consultation.

01How do I know if my home needs more insulation?

Cold rooms, drafts, high heating bills, and an attic where you can see the tops of the ceiling joists are all signs.

02Can you insulate walls without opening them?

Often, yes. Insulation can be blown into closed walls through small holes that are patched afterward.

03Are there rebates for insulation?

Some utilities offer rebates for insulation and air sealing. Check with yours; we can provide the project details they ask for.

04Are you licensed, insured, and bonded?

Yes. Julius Remodeling Group is a licensed, insured, and bonded Washington general contractor (License JULIURG780NJ). Verify it with Washington L&I.

Process

A simple process. Better results.

Better planning, better building, better results. Four of the six steps happen before anything is torn out.

  1. 01

    Free consultation

    We meet and discuss your ideas, needs, priorities, and budget.

  2. 02

    Design & planning

    We develop the scope, design, material selections, and project plan.

  3. 03

    Estimate & agreement

    You receive a clear proposal outlining the scope, pricing, and payment schedule.

  4. 04

  6. 05

FAQ

Whole home questions.

Anything else, ask us at your consultation.

01Can we stay in the house during the remodel?

It depends on the scope. We'll talk it through at your consultation and, where it makes sense, plan the work in phases so part of the house stays usable.

02Do you handle permits?

Yes. We coordinate permits when required and prepare your project for construction before demolition begins.

03Do you also remodel condos?

Yes. Full-unit condo and apartment renovations follow the same process, plus the building approvals. See how condo remodeling works.

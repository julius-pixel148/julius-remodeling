FAQ

Addition questions.

Anything else, ask us at your consultation.

01What is a bump-out?

A bump-out is a small addition, often just a few feet deep, that extends one room, such as a kitchen or bathroom. It adds space without the scope of a full room addition.

02Will the addition match my house?

That's the goal. We match the roofline, siding, windows, and interior finishes wherever possible, so the addition doesn't look added on.

03Do I need a permit for an addition?

Yes. Additions need permits and inspections. We prepare the drawings and handle the permit process for you.

04Are you licensed, insured, and bonded?

Yes. Julius Remodeling Group is a licensed, insured, and bonded Washington general contractor (License JULIURG780NJ). Verify it with Washington L&I.

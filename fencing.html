FAQ

Fence questions.

Anything else, ask us at your consultation.

01How tall can my fence be?

It depends on your city and where the fence sits on your lot; front yards usually have lower limits. We check the rules for your address.

02Do I need to know my property line?

Yes. We build along the line you confirm, and a survey is the surest way to know where it is.

03Can you remove my old fence?

Yes. Removing and hauling away the old fence is part of a replacement.

04Are you licensed, insured, and bonded?

Yes. Julius Remodeling Group is a licensed, insured, and bonded Washington general contractor (License JULIURG780NJ). Verify it with Washington L&I.

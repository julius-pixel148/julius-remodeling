Your options

Refresh, remodel, or convert.

Every bathroom starts in a different place. We'll look at yours at the consultation and recommend the level of work that fits your goals and budget.

Option 01

Bathroom refresh

Keep the layout and the shower, and update what you see: a new vanity, counter, mirror, lighting, fixtures, and paint. A big change with the least disruption.

  • Same layout
  • New vanity, lighting, fixtures
  • Fastest option
Option 02

Tub-to-shower conversion

Replace an old tub with a walk-in shower: new waterproofing, custom tile, niches, a bench if you want one, and glass. Easier to use every day.

  • Walk-in shower
  • Custom tile and niches
  • Easier daily access
Option 03

Full remodel

Down to the studs: new layout if needed, plumbing, waterproofing, tile, shower or tub, vanity, lighting, and ventilation. Everything new, behind the walls and in front of them.

  • New everything
  • Layout changes possible
  • Plumbing and electrical updated

FAQ

Bathroom questions.

Anything else, ask us at your consultation.

01Do you build walk-in showers?

Yes. Walk-in showers with custom tile, niches, and glass are one of our most requested projects. You can see two of them above.

02Can you convert my tub into a shower?

Yes. Tub-to-shower conversions include removing the tub, waterproofing the new shower area, custom tile, and glass.

03Why does waterproofing matter so much?

Water that gets behind tile causes damage you don't see until it's expensive. Waterproofing is one of our specialties, and it's done before any tile goes on.

04Can you remodel just one bathroom?

Yes. Whether it's a single powder room or every bathroom in the house, the process is the same: organized, transparent, and carefully executed.

05Do you remodel condo bathrooms?

Yes, including the HOA approvals, water shutoffs, and building rules that come with it. See how condo remodeling works.

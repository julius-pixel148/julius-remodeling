FAQ

Door questions.

Anything else, ask us at your consultation.

01Do you replace just the door or the frame too?

Either. If the frame is in good shape, we can replace the door itself. If not, we install a new pre-hung door with its own frame.

02Can you change every interior door in the house?

Yes. Updating all interior doors and hardware to one style is one of the most noticeable upgrades you can make.

03Do you paint and trim the new doors?

Yes. Casing, trim, and paint are included, so the door matches the rest of the room.

04Are you licensed, insured, and bonded?

Yes. Julius Remodeling Group is a licensed, insured, and bonded Washington general contractor (License JULIURG780NJ). Verify it with Washington L&I.

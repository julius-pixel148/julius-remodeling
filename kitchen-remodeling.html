Your options

Refresh, replace, or go fully custom.

Not every kitchen needs to be gutted. We'll walk through your kitchen at the consultation and help you pick the level that fits your goals and budget.

Option 01

Repaint existing cabinets

If your cabinet boxes are solid, we can repaint them and add new hardware for a completely new look, without the cost of new cabinetry. It pairs well with new countertops, backsplash, and lighting.

  • Keep your current layout
  • New color, new hardware
  • Least disruption
Option 02

Semi-custom cabinets

Factory-built cabinets in standard sizes, with a wide choice of door styles, finishes, and modifications. A strong balance of quality, choice, and budget when old cabinets need replacing.

  • Replace worn or dated cabinets
  • Many styles and finishes
  • Layout changes possible
Option 03

Custom cabinets

Built to your exact dimensions, layout, and finish. Best for unusual spaces, floor-to-ceiling storage, and an island designed around how you cook and gather.

  • Built to exact measurements
  • Any finish, any detail
  • Full layout freedom

FAQ

Kitchen questions.

Anything else, ask us at your consultation.

01Should I repaint my cabinets or replace them?

If the cabinet boxes are solid and you like the layout, repainting with new hardware is the most budget-friendly way to transform the kitchen. If they are worn, water-damaged, or the layout does not work, replacing them with semi-custom or custom cabinets makes more sense. We will give you an honest recommendation at the consultation.

02What is the difference between semi-custom and custom cabinets?

Semi-custom cabinets come in standard sizes with many styles, finishes, and modifications. Custom cabinets are built to your exact dimensions and design, which suits unusual spaces, full-height storage, and one-of-a-kind islands. See the comparison above.

03Do you handle the electrical and plumbing?

Yes. Electrical and plumbing work is managed by our team under our license, including new circuits, lighting, sink and appliance hookups, and moving lines for a new layout. Permits are coordinated when required.

04Can you change my kitchen's layout?

Yes. Layout changes are worked out during design and planning, including any plumbing and electrical moves, and permits are coordinated when required.

05Do you remodel condo kitchens?

Yes. Kitchens inside buildings also need HOA approvals, elevator bookings, and building rules handled. See how condo remodeling works.

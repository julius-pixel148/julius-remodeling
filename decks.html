FAQ

Deck questions.

Anything else, ask us at your consultation.

01Composite or wood decking?

Composite costs more up front but doesn't need staining and resists rot. Wood costs less and looks natural but needs regular maintenance in our climate.

02Can you replace the boards but keep the frame?

Sometimes. If the framing is sound, we can replace just the decking and railings. We inspect the frame first and tell you honestly.

03Do I need a permit for a deck?

Many decks do, especially ones attached to the house or above a certain height. We check your city's rules and handle the permit.

04Are you licensed, insured, and bonded?

Yes. Julius Remodeling Group is a licensed, insured, and bonded Washington general contractor (License JULIURG780NJ). Verify it with Washington L&I.

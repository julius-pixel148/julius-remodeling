FAQ

Condo questions.

Anything else, ask us at your consultation.

01What makes condo and apartment remodeling different?

Working inside a building means understanding more than construction. Building requirements, HOA coordination, elevator access, material deliveries, working-hour restrictions, noise requirements, plumbing shutoffs, protection of common areas, and limited workspace all need to be planned before construction begins. We specialize in managing those details.

02Do you work with HOAs and building management?

Yes. We handle the paperwork and coordination most buildings require, including approvals, certificates of insurance, elevator reservations, and work-hour rules, before any work starts.

03Can you remodel just one room in my unit?

Yes. Whether you're renovating a single bathroom or the whole unit, the process is the same: organized, transparent, and carefully executed.

04Are you licensed, insured, and bonded?

Yes. Julius Remodeling Group is a licensed, insured, and bonded Washington general contractor (License JULIURG780NJ), and you can verify it with Washington L&I.
